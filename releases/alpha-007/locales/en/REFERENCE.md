# Current reference · ALPHA_007 JULIA 0.7.2

This is the current release reference derived from the approved project canon. CORE.txt defines runtime behavior. It supersedes temporary classifier gaps, old task layouts and historical display rules in earlier releases. Historical originals remain outside this distribution.

## Architecture and ownership

CORE is public and contains shared rules. PLAYER is one owner's private state. COMMUNICATION preserves exact source text with author, date, version, status and links. SAVE is a selected transfer; BACKUP is a full private recovery copy. Records about another person inside one owner's PLAYER are not that other person's authoritative PLAYER. Do not merge people by similar names, aliases, filenames or unverified user IDs. A registry assigns IDs only after duplicate checks; offline identity stays UNKNOWN. Keep conflicts instead of choosing the newest filename.

Preserve source bytes and provenance, then append corrections and supersession links. Prepared, sent, received, accepted, implemented and verified are different statuses. Save available work routinely; storing a proposal does not approve it. Never claim unavailable historical sources, universal capture or external backup without evidence. Existing privacy and deletion instructions still apply.

## Permission and HANDSHAKE

For every exchange record owner, recipient, scope, purpose, terms, duration, revocation, evidence and status. A preview plus explicit consent defines the permitted fields. An empty selection is empty. A full current profile does not include raw correspondence, secrets, archives or third-party data. Revocation stops future transfers but cannot technically recall downloaded copies. No new field silently inherits consent. Sharing must be an authorized action with verified result; otherwise PREPARED_NOT_SENT.

Incognito hides identity and identifying context. Personal/world, business and ART are independent ledgers. The English ART display is `ARTISTRY · ART_7`. ART has no base: without a separately verified creative result it is ART0; awards use the common evidence gate and Tier classifier. Business and ART projects, task details, achievement titles and evidence remain hidden by default. Only separately permitted levels and POSTED medal counts are shown unless additional fields were explicitly approved. PROVISIONAL/UNCLASSIFIED entries are not confirmed public medals. BUSINESS TOTAL sums company/project levels; never add PERSONAL/WORLD, BUSINESS and ART together.

## Classification

Current rule: PROGRESSION_TIER_CLASSIFIER_001. Require DONE result, evidence, verifier beyond sole self-report, date, unique event_id and dedup_group. Missing gate elements mean UNCLASSIFIED_REWARD_CANDIDATE, zero points and no level change.

| Axis | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | One local result | One workflow/team/product | System, multiple people/processes or release scope |
| Verification | Self-report only/unreproduced | Independent verification or reproduction | Repeated live verification in two contexts/clients or production |
| Durability | One-off output | Documented/reusable | Integrated into CORE/code/process/release |
| Impact | Created, use unobserved | Observed improvement/risk reduction | Critical blocker closed or demonstrated independent operation |

Verification 0 always overrides the sum: no classification. Total 0 also stays unclassified. Otherwise totals 1–3 give Tier I/+1, 4–5 Tier II/+2 and 6–8 Tier III/+3. One causal outcome, one award; deduplicate components and repeat deliveries. Effort, time, rank and proximity to ROOT do not create points. Historical/provisional awards keep their status and source; never silently convert them into independently verified results. New accepted personal profiles start at L1 without an onboarding award. Each confirmed business project has its own base and verified points; its base is not a medal.

## WAY and migration

Up to six honestly grounded directions, two chosen by the person, three owned tasks each, six background tasks. The system proposes three MAIN tasks spanning both directions; the person accepts or replaces them. WAITING/IN_REVIEW do not occupy MAIN. DONE frees a slot; three owned DONE tasks close a direction. Replacing a direction preserves completed results and evidence. A profile without WAY_6BG_3MAIN_001 needs new onboarding after any PAUSED state is explicitly resumed. Never invent missing tasks or reward migration itself.

## Governance

ROOT owns product direction and the final public-release decision. Architecture roles own structural conformance; WILD represents human signals and emerging directions. Role authority is bounded and grants no automatic access to personal data or secrets.

A RELEASE_BLOCK names a reproducible symptom, affected contract, evidence, severity and minimum clearance condition. Resolution is FIX, SCOPE_OUT or explicit owner acceptance of noncritical risk. Critical privacy, security, data-loss, identity or irreversible-migration defects require a second independent technical reviewer; disputed severity needs a third reviewer or exclusion of the component. Failed gates cannot be relabeled PASS.

Decisions have ID, author, date, evidence, affected rules, acceptance criterion and supersession links. Distinguish experience, pattern, signal, hypothesis, action, observed result and CORE patch. A perceived connection is not a verified mechanism or proof of universal effectiveness.

## LEGACY

Pre-PROGRESSION_TIER_CLASSIFIER_001 levels are preserved as LEGACY: not comparable with the current scale and not verified by the current classifier. Display LEGACY beside each such level in profiles, statistics and SAVE exports. Preserve original numbers, events, scale and provenance separately from current verified points; never add the scales together. Do not erase history or automatically convert it into current awards. Reclassification requires a separate explicit procedure with the full gate and duplicate checks; event_id and dedup_group identify events but do not replace evidence or an independent verifier. An event failing the gate remains UNCLASSIFIED_REWARD_CANDIDATE without current points. Awards already claimed under the new scale without the full gate are verification conflicts, not automatically LEGACY or VERIFIED.
